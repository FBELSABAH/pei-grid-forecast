03 / A clock before a model

18:00 Atlantic

Issue the next local day's hourly forecast, including 23- and 25-hour DST days.

One-hour buffer

Use load intervals ending by 17:00. Electricity publication latency is assumed; historical release vintages are unavailable.

Separate windows

Select the model on 2024 development data. Calibrate intervals on earlier, separate data. Keep the original method fixed. Report the post-audit monthly revision separately as exploratory.

February 2025 has particularly low coverage. No target interpolation. Missing-field causes can overlap; details are in monthly eligibility CSV. Results do not establish performance during excluded periods.

Quality checks

  • Reject invalid and nonpositive load readings.
  • Flag repeated readings only once four hours have elapsed; gaps over 30 minutes reset the run. Never reject earlier readings using a future run end.
  • Require at least three observations spanning 30 minutes per accepted hour.
  • Never interpolate target demand. Score all methods on the same eligible hours.
  • Fingerprint source files, features and prediction outputs.

What this cannot establish

  • Actual bill savings, emissions reductions or usable flexible capacity.
  • Summerside-only demand from province-wide observations.
  • Grid safety, operating limits or outage causes.
  • Performance during excluded poor-data periods.
  • Independent prospective accuracy: this is retrospective testing on downloaded archives.
